Privacy Policy for Pulse

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Pulse ("the App", "we", "us") is a daily-challenge, streak, and achievement app developed by BrandStack. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Pulse, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Pulse, we collect:

  • Account information: the email address and display name you provide when you sign up (via email/password or a supported social sign-in).
  • App content and activity: your daily challenge completions, streaks, XP, level, and unlocked achievements — this is the core data that makes the app work.
  • Device and usage analytics: anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as which screens are viewed and how features are used) collected via Firebase Analytics, to help us understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: automatically collected via Firebase Crashlytics when the App encounters an error, to help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Push notification token: a device token used solely to deliver reminders and updates you've opted into, via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We do not knowingly collect payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Pulse account and profile
  • To track and display your streaks, XP, levels, and achievements
  • To send you optional reminder notifications
  • To understand app usage and diagnose crashes, so we can improve stability and features

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Pulse is built on Google Firebase, and the services below process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages account sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile, streak, and achievement data
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and diagnostic reporting
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery

You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention

We retain your account and app data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from within the App, your profile and associated app data are removed from our systems, other than data we are required to retain for legal or security purposes (such as fraud-prevention records), and anonymized analytics that can no longer be tied to you.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and associated data directly within the App, from the Settings screen.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time from your device settings or in-app.
  • Access or correction: you can contact us at any time (see below) to request a copy of your data or ask us to correct it.

Children's Privacy

Pulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
